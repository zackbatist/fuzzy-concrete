# fuzzy-concrete
This repository contains the source for my paper titled _Balancing Situated and Objective Representations in Archaeological Fieldwork_, which is published in _Advances in Archaeological Practice_.
A rendered preprint containing near-identical content (save for some minor spelling and grammatical differences, and including animated versions of some figures) can be accessed at [zackbatist.info/fuzzy-concrete](http://zackbatist.info/fuzzy-concrete).

When citing this work, please refer to the version of record: [10.1017/aap.2025.10101](https://doi.org/10.1017/aap.2025.10101).

## Abstract
Archaeology comprises both systematic and pragmatic attitudes and processes concerned with the collection and maintenance of data.
Thus, it needs to obtain formally defined data while also grappling with the fuzzy and uncertain nature of archaeological encounters, especially in fieldwork environments.
This produces an epistemic tension, as archaeologists struggle to reconcile their desire to produce concrete outcomes based on objective facts and their intuitive understanding that data are in fact products of situated decisions and actions.
Through observations of archaeological practices, interviews with archaeologists at work, and analysis of the documents they produced while recording objects of archaeological concern, this article describes how archaeologists cope with this tension and integrate it into their work experiences.
